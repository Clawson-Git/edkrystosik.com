# Audity

Audity is SaaS built for boutique AI consulting firms (3-25 people) running client
work as a team. It removes the capacity ceiling that caps most firms at 6-8 audits
a year, so partners, principals, and practice leads can scale advisory engagements
without scaling headcount. Ed Krystosik is co-founder.

## Who it's for
Boutique AI consulting firms (3-25 people) that deliver AI audits and transformation
advisory as a team and want to take on more client work without hiring more people.

## What you get
- Audit automation that cuts delivery time by 60%+
- Multi-seat collaboration built for how firms actually run engagements
- A repeatable methodology for AI transformation audits
